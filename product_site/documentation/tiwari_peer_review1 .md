## Content & Audience
### Did your peers clearly understand your site’s purpose and target audience?
Yes, they clearly understood my site's purpose and target audience.

### What suggestions did they make to improve your site’s content or messaging?
One peer observed how there were 3 links that all led to the account creation page, and so I removed one of them because it was redundant.

## Design & User Experience
### How did your peers respond to your site’s design (layout, color, typography, or organization)?
They said they really like the dark theme I had going and they liked the overall layout.

### Did they find the design effective in communicating your message and engaging users?
Yes, they found the design to look very professional and easy for the users to understand and engage in.

## Functionality & Performance

### What issues did your peers identify with features or interactivity on your site?
One suggestion that was provided was to fix the sidebar not properly adjusting when the resolution got smaller. It would end up breaking the design and look very unappealing. Another issue found was one of the links in the header was disappearing when they were in the "How to Sell Books" page.

### Did they note any pages or elements that loaded slowly or inconsistently across devices or browsers?
No, none of the elements were slow or loaded inconsistently across browsers.

## Accessibility & Usability

### What feedback did you receive about accessibility (e.g., alt text, color contrast, keyboard navigation)?
One suggestion that was made was to make the background in the header a different color because it was hard to see the words on the logo (I'm unsure if I want to change that as I and other peers like it). They said the navigation was good and the color contrast was good as well.

### What can you improve to make your site more inclusive and user-friendly?
Improving the logo's color contrast by either removing the words or changing the logo colors. Or changing the background color of the header.

## Action Plan

### What did your peers like most about your site?
My peers really enjoyed my dark minimalist design, the layout, and the easy navigation.

### What two improvements will you make based on their feedback, and how will you implement them?
One improvement I will make is fixing the missing header link in the "How To Sell Books" page by simply copying the header in the welcome page and pasting it there. I will also fix the sidebar resolution issue by increasing the max width from 600 to 1300.

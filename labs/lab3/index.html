My Favorite Dish: Momo

Momo is my favorite dish because I grew up eating it in my home.
A special memory I have with it is from my childhood where my
best friend and I would eat it while watching Minecraft videos.
It is created using a dough wrapper that is filled with meat or vegetables,
I always preferred the chicken filling.


Ingredients Amount
All purpose flour 1 cup
Water 1/2 cup
Vegetable oil 1-2 tsp per momo
Minced Chicken 1 lb
Onion 1/2 cup
